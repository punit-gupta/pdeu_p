Research & engineering focus

My primary research interests span Cybersecurity, AI for Security & Security for AI, and Remote Work enabling Technologies. I also have a keen interest in Computer Algorithms & Problem Solving, Application Networking, Large Scale Distributed Cloud Services, and Monitoring & Observability[reference:1].

At Microsoft, I build systems related to AI for Security & Security for AI, Secure Service Edge (SSE), and Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) to enable remote work while improving the cybersecurity posture of organizations. If you're one of the billions of users of Microsoft's Copilot Studio, Office365, Outlook.com, Windows, Foundry, or Azure, you're interacting with and staying secure through solutions I'm leading at Microsoft[reference:2].

Previously at Meta (formerly Facebook), I worked in the Monitoring & Observability domain, building distributed Log Ingestion, Indexing, Storage, Querying & Analytics systems that enable centralized logging for thousands of services at petabyte scale. At Citrix, I worked extensively on Application Networking, Clientless-VPN, SSLVPN, Content Filtering & URL Categorization and helped launch Citrix's very first SSE solution[reference:3].
